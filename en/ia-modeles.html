A model, an AI: what are we talking about?

People often confuse "an AI" with "a model". In reality, what we commonly call an AI — ChatGPT, Le Chat, Perplexity — is an interface. Behind it runs one or several models. The diagram below shows who does what across the whole landscape.

The model

It is the engine, the part that "thinks". A giant neural network trained on massive corpora of text and data. A model is a file of weights — from a few gigabytes to several hundred. If it is open, you can download it and run it yourself; if it is closed, only its publisher can run it.

The interface

It is what you see: the chat, the search bar, the voice assistant. An AI in the everyday sense is almost always an interface talking to one or more models — its own, or other people's.

The link between the two

Some companies do everything: their model, their interface (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, DeepSeek). Others only assemble: an elegant interface plugged into other people's models (Perplexity, Venice, Poe). And the same model can power several competing interfaces.

Who does what: the big diagram

Models on top, interfaces below, one arrow for every link. Solid arrows: the in-house engine. Dotted golden arrows: a third-party model brought in. Count the arrows entering Perplexity or Venice: that is what an aggregator is.

  • The in-house model powers its own interface
  • The interface uses a third-party model
  • Open models, downloadable by anyone

State of the landscape as of 3 November 2026 — open/closed statuses drift fast: they are dated.

Open or closed: four philosophies

Few companies publish everything or nothing. Catalogues are mixed — the real question is which part of the house stays behind the door.

1

Open by principle

Even the flagship model is published. Its weights directly power other interfaces.

2

Open core with tiers

Open core (Apache 2.0), commercial tiers above a revenue threshold.

3

Closed at the top, open downstream

The frontier stays closed while distribution lines are opened — to flood the ecosystem without giving away the flagship.

4

Closed

No downloadable weights today: these models only run on the publisher's servers.

A model's status is a dated fact: Mistral opened its Medium, Alibaba closed its Max, xAI re-closed after Grok-1.

Zoom: Perplexity and Venice

Perplexity

An augmented search engine. Its Sonar model (a Llama derivative) lives alongside GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Kimi: the diagram above shows the six arrows coming in.

Venice

No in-house model: Venice routes open models across decentralized GPUs, with zero retention — nothing is stored on their servers.

The rule to remember: your data stays where the model runs. Choosing an AI means choosing who runs the model — the interface's looks say nothing about that destination.
