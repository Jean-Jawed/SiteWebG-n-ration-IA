Why this site?

Generative AI is everywhere — in the news, in our tools, in our jobs. But between the media hype and the technical jargon, it is hard to know what is actually happening. Génération-IA takes a clear, well-documented look at the whole ecosystem: what a model is, who runs what, what it costs, and what it really changes — at work, in school, in creative work.
